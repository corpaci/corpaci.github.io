Self-reference is one move with very different outcomes: recursion and quines put it to work, the Liar and Gödel show where it collapses, and Goodhart, ELK, and probe contamination are the same shapes wearing machine learning. Each node below is one instance, scored on five features (mechanism, productivity, what breaks, locus, domain). Solid lines are hand-labeled relations; dashed lines are computed affinities ; pairs whose feature distance is small. Click a node for its story; drag to untangle.

The productive→collapse spectrum here is the same territory as the limits-of-formalization post and the limits/ interactive: the walls (Gödel, Tarski, Rice) sit in the middle of the map, the alignment cluster inherits them, and the productive cluster is the existence proof that self-reference is not always fatal. Distances are weighted Jaccard over the feature sets ; weights and data in self_reference.json.
