This is the first post on this site, and it seems right to say what it is for.

<!--more-->

I study and build certificate-backed methods for detecting semantic drift in AI-generated specifications, proofs, and other artifacts. I currently mentor research at MARS V and SPS Apart, participate in CAISH’s Hardware Assurance Program, and am completing an MSc in Data Science at TU Wien. My background includes hardware verification at AMD.

I mean this place for thinking in public. Some posts will be technical and others will be more reflective.

I call the thread that connects many of these things "systems all the way down", a sort of fractalic representation of reality. The same structural patterns appear whether we are looking at silicon, neural networks, mathematical spaces, or the architecture of attention itself. This site is where I map that thread.

What you can expect:

- **Technical writings** into formalization limits, SAE features, bias measurement, and the mathematics behind them.
- **Conceptual sketches** connecting formal methods and interpretability.
- **Research notes** that are somewhere between a lab notebook and an essay.

If that resonates, I would be glad to hear from you ; [contact](/about/#contact).
